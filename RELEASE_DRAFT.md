# Update — v1.7

## New
- Center Directors get a new Dashboard tab. It opens with a welcome banner, your center's calendar, and a Check-ins graph.
- The Check-ins graph shows how many ninjas came in each week. Hover any point on the line to see the exact count for that week. Open it up and you can switch between a week, a month, six months, or all time, see your busiest day, your weekly pace, and how you're tracking against the period before.
- Quick tiles on the Dashboard get you to Reports, Curriculum, and What's New in one tap.
- Add events to your center's calendar right from the Dashboard. Game builds, tournaments, parents nights, whatever is coming up. Give it a name, date, time, and a type, and your instructors see it. Tap any day on the month grid to add something, tap an event to edit or remove it.
- Ninja birthdays show up on that calendar too. Every ninja with a birthday on file gets a cake on their day, and tapping the name opens their profile. Busy day? Tap the "more" line to see everything on it.
- Instructors get an Upcoming events list on their own dashboard so they always know what is next at the center.
- Leave sticky notes on the Dashboard for yourself or the other directors at your center. Pick a color, format them with bold and bullet lists, edit them, clear them whenever.
- Change your display name anytime from your Account page, right above your username.
- Turn on Experimental features in Account to unlock the theme and color picker. Choose an accent color and set light or dark. This used to be hidden behind a secret tap, now it lives in one place.

## Bug Fixes
- Logged a club today and it said "Yesterday"? Fixed. It reads "Today" now.
- A club's pinned note no longer shows a stray date next to the button.
- Marking a ninja present in a club now counts as activity. They won't show up under No Check-Ins on Reports just because their sessions were all clubs, and their last club date shows as their most recent activity.

## Changes
- Club cards look better now. Each one gets a colored header (or its cover photo, if you've added one), and the club name is bigger so it's easier to read.
- For Center Directors, Reports moved off the sidebar. It's a tile on the new Dashboard, and there's still a link on the Check-ins card.
- Roadmap is no longer in the sidebar. Get to it from the Curriculum tile on the Dashboard, or the View Roadmap button on any ninja's profile.
- The two floating buttons at the top of the phone screen are gone, so pages get the full screen.
- The Center Director Dashboard got a cleaner look. Your sticky notes and the calendar run down the main column, with the quick tiles and Check-ins alongside.
- The Enrollment ring came off the Dashboard. That program breakdown already lives on Reports, so there's no reason to read it twice.
- Buttons and menus feel a little snappier. Buttons give a small press when you tap them, and pop-ups open with a smoother motion.
- Reports got a visual refresh. Belt Distribution is now a ranked list with each belt's icon and a bar that shows at a glance which belts have the most ninjas, with the top belt called out. Enrollment shows each program's logo, and there's a new Belt-Ups count for the last 30 days up top.
