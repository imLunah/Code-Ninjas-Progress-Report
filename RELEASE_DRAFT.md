# Update v2.0


## New

- The Parent Portal has been completely redesigned. Parents will be able to view their child's progress through a refined experience. Instructor view will be updated soon.

- Re-designed onboarding process for parents. CDs must provide center code for parents to sign in.

- Parent portal homepage shows live ninja count which is calculated when a ninja is checked in on Dojolink

- Edit Profile is your ID card now. Tap your name to retype it right on the card, tap your photo to slide through the avatars, and tap the card to turn it over.

- Clicking a staff member hands you their ID card, with their progress logs on a sheet of paper tucked behind it.

- When logging CREATE, adding an additional project now has its own belt and level.

- Event listings. Center Directors get an Events page for promoting what's happening at the center.

- Families see your events. Published listings show as a banner at the top of the Parent Portal home.

- Hovering Dashboard in the nav opens a quick menu  (STILL WIP)

- Every CREATE ninja gets a sticker book to earn achievements which shows what they learned and how rare it is.

- Refined Dojolink landing page

## Changes

- Logging a session takes you back to Today's Board. If a ninja is checked into more than one class, or has make up sessions to catch up on, the page stays where it is and moves you to the next class so you can finish them in one sitting.

- The MyStudio sign in expires after about 24 hours on their end, and DojoLink now says so.

## Bug Fixes

- Session notes were written once per class, but printed under every project. Logging two projects in one sitting showed the note twice, which read as two sessions that happened to say the same thing. It prints once now.

- Switching to another class while logging kept the note you had written for the previous class sitting in the box, which could submit one class's note under another. Switching starts a clean note now.

- For admin accounts, the floating view switcher pill could sit on top of open dialogs and cover what they were saying.
