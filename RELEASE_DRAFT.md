# Update — v1.7

## New
- Center Directors get a new Dashboard tab. It opens with the date and a greeting, your notes, your center's calendar, and a Check-ins graph.
- The Check-ins graph shows how many ninjas came in each week. Hover any point on the line to see the exact count for that week. The card also lists your busiest day, how many ninjas a week you are averaging, and your eight week total. Open it up and you can switch between a week, a month, six months, or all time.
- Links under the greeting get you to Reports, Curriculum, and What's New in one tap.
- Add events to your center's calendar right from the Dashboard. Game builds, tournaments, parents nights, whatever is coming up. Give it a name, date, time, and a type, and your instructors see it. Tap any day on the month grid to add something, tap an event to edit or remove it.
- Ninja birthdays show up on that calendar too. Every ninja with a birthday on file gets a cake on their day, and tapping the name opens their profile. Busy day? Tap the "more" line to see everything on it.
- Instructors get an Upcoming events list on their own dashboard so they always know what is next at the center.
- Leave notes on the Dashboard for yourself or the other directors at your center. Pick a color, format them with bold and bullet lists, edit them, clear them whenever.
- Drag your notes around to put them in the order you want. They shuffle out of the way as you move one, and wherever you leave them is where everyone at your center sees them. Every note is the same size now, so the board stays tidy no matter how much you write on one.
- Change your display name anytime from your Account page, right above your username.
- Turn on Experimental features in Account to unlock the theme and color picker. Choose an accent color and set light or dark. This used to be hidden behind a secret tap, now it lives in one place.

## Bug Fixes
- Usernames could be created with spaces and other characters in them, which made them awkward to type at sign-in. They now have to be letters, numbers, dots, underscores or hyphens, with no spaces, and the Account page says so under the field. One existing account had a space in its username and has been updated, so if signing in stops working for you, check with your director for the new one.
- Signing in no longer trips over a stray space. If your keyboard or password manager adds one to the end of your username, you still get in.
- If saving club attendees or deleting a session failed while you had DojoLink open from your home screen, nothing happened on screen and you had no idea it had gone wrong. It tells you now.
- Logged a club today and it said "Yesterday"? Fixed. It reads "Today" now.
- A club's pinned note no longer shows a stray date next to the button.
- Marking a ninja present in a club now counts as activity. They won't show up under No Check-Ins on Reports just because their sessions were all clubs, and their last club date shows as their most recent activity.

## Changes
- Club cards look better now. Each one gets a colored header (or its cover photo, if you've added one), and the club name is bigger so it's easier to read.
- For Center Directors, Reports moved off the sidebar. It's a link under the greeting on the new Dashboard.
- Roadmap is no longer in the sidebar. Get to it from the Curriculum link on the Dashboard, or the View Roadmap button on any ninja's profile.
- The two floating buttons at the top of the phone screen are gone, so pages get the full screen.
- The Center Director Dashboard got a cleaner look. The greeting sits at the top of the page instead of inside a box, your notes run the full width below it, and the calendar and Check-ins sit side by side under that.
- The Enrollment ring came off the Dashboard. That program breakdown already lives on Reports, so there's no reason to read it twice.
- Buttons and menus feel a little snappier. Buttons give a small press when you tap them, and pop-ups open with a smoother motion.
- Pages that are still loading now show the shape of what's coming instead of the word Loading. The roster, clubs, reports, ninja and club profiles, staff, curriculum and the parent portal all hold their layout while they load, so nothing jumps around when it arrives.
- The Check-ins graph follows the accent color you picked in Appearance. It used to always be blue.
- Getting around with a keyboard works properly everywhere now. Whatever you're on is outlined as you tab, in your accent color, on every page.
- Pop-ups close with the Escape key, and tabbing inside one stays inside it instead of wandering onto the page behind. When you close it you land back on the button you opened it with.
- Your Account page is laid out like a proper settings screen on a computer. Your account and preferences are grouped down the left and you pick one at a time, instead of every setting stacked in one long column. Phones keep the single scroll.
- Dark mode is more consistent. Cards across Reports, the parent portal, Clubs and Settings now sit off the page the same way the Dashboard's already did.
- Login, the landing page and the parent pages no longer shift around when your phone's address bar hides and reappears.
- The Account settings menu stays in place on a computer while the panel beside it scrolls, so you can always see the sections and Sign Out.
- The staff count above the staff list is gone. It was counting the rows you were already looking at.
- Small colored badges around the version number in What's New, a staff member's role, the centers they cover, and the current module on the Roadmap have been dropped. That text reads on its own without a tinted pill around it.
- Reports got a visual refresh. Belt Distribution is now a ranked list with each belt's icon and a bar that shows at a glance which belts have the most ninjas, with the top belt called out. Enrollment shows each program's logo, and there's a new Belt-Ups count for the last 30 days up top.

## Security
- Usernames are matched the same way everywhere you sign in. It used to be possible to create two accounts whose names differed only by capital letters, and a sign-in could then land on either one. That can't happen now.
