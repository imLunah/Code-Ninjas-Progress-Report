# Update v1.7 - pretty big update and cleaning up things

## New
- New Dashboard tab for Center Directors! It comes with sticky notes you can drag around.
- Check in graph are also on the dashboard. It shows how many ninjas came in each week, and hovering a point gives you the exact count. The card also carries your busiest day, how many ninjas a week you are averaging, and your eight week total. 
- Reports, Curriculum and What's New have been moved towards the dashboard.
- Put events on your center's calendar! Game buildings, parents nights, whatever is coming up. Give it a name, date, time and type, and your instructors see it.
- Every ninja with a birthday on file gets added into the calendar
- Instructors get the center calendar on their own board. Tap the calendar button on Today's Board and it opens over the page.
- Resources tab has been added on the Curriculum page! Every program has a resource section that will be populated in the future. So far, I have added the completion passwords for AI and Robotics Academy.
- Change your display name whenever you like, from your Account page right above your username.
- Experimental features in profile settings. I want to make interesting features that may / may not break design

## Bug fixes
- Fixed a bug where Senseis did not receive the updated ninja cards from the previous update.
- Fixed a bug where users can sign up their profile with a space in the name
- Fixed a bug where logging a club would show yesterday's date instead.
- Removed stray date from appearing next to club notes
- Fixed a bug where a club attendance would not count as a session which made the reports page not accurate.
- Fixed a bug where two usernames with the same name, but different capitalization would still form as different accounts

## Changes
- Signing in will take Center Directors to the Dashboard instead of Today's Board.
- Curriculum has been rebuilt. Programs are tabs across the top, and every module is a row you open to see its lessons, now numbered. The colored buttons, tinted boxes and count pills are gone, so it reads as something you look things up in. CREATE's belts sit under the program tabs with the belt icon on each one.
- Club cards look better. Each one gets a colored header, or its cover photo if you've added one, and the club name is bigger so it's easier to read.
- Reports moved off the Center Director sidebar. It's a link under the greeting on the Dashboard.
- Reports got a visual refresh. Belt Distribution runs down the belts in order, each with its icon and a bar showing how many ninjas are on it, so you can see where your center sits at a glance. Enrollment does the same with each program's logo. Hover a bar for the exact count. There's also a new Belt-Ups count for the last 30 days up top.
- The two floating buttons at the top of the phone screen are gone.
- Buttons and menus feel snappier. Buttons give a small press when you tap them, and pop-ups open with a smoother motion.
- Improved loading page by adding skeleton screens instead of a traditional "loading" status.
- Keyboard navigation works properly everywhere.
- Pop-ups close with the Escape key. Tabbing inside one stays inside it instead of wandering onto the page behind, and when you close it you land back on the button you opened it with.
- Re-designed account profile page for a cleaner and easier panel.
- the landing page and the parent pages no longer shift around when your phone's address bar hides and reappears.
- Removed staff count from staff page.
